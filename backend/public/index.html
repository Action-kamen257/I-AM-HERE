Core Capabilities

Built for Real-World Survival

Engineered with strict zero-failure principles. Offline queuing, verifiable carrier delivery, and low-power location tracking.

🚨

Zero-Delay SOS Dispatch

Trigger distress alerts through on-screen panic button, triple-shake accelerometer motion, or 4 consecutive power button clicks without unlocking the screen.

📍

Live GPS Breadcrumb Trail

Continuous GPS coordinate streaming over secure WebSockets with sub-meter accuracy, speed calculation, battery telemetry, and offline SQLite buffering.

🛡️

Evidence Delivery Protocol

Eliminates false hope. Displays separate states for Carrier SMS Sent, Delivered to Handset, Seen by Guardian, and Responding with live ETA.

🤫

Duress Cancellation Mode

If forced to enter a cancellation PIN under duress, entering the alternate Duress PIN visually cancels the on-screen alarm while covertly continuing live tracking.

📞

112 & Police CAD Relay

Structured emergency data packets pre-formatted for dispatchers containing exact coordinates, victim description, and contact telephone numbers.

🚶‍♀️

Walk Safe Journey Guard

Set a destination and estimated transit time. If you do not check in by the scheduled arrival window, the app automatically alerts your guardian circle.
