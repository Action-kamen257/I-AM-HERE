# Google Play Store Listing Specification

## App Title
**I AM HERE: Emergency Women Safety**

## Short Description (80 characters max)
Real-time emergency distress network with live tracking & trusted contact alerts

## Full Description
**I AM HERE** is an uncompromising emergency safety platform engineered specifically to protect women in moments of acute danger. Unlike conventional alert apps that send a blind message and leave you wondering if anyone heard you, **I AM HERE** provides a transparent 5-stage contact evidence engine that keeps you informed in real time.

### Why I AM HERE is Different: The Evidence Engine
When you trigger an alert, our system tracks and displays platform-verified delivery states for every trusted contact in your circle:
• **Queued**: Distress signal is prepared and priority-routed.
• **Sent**: Carrier-verified transmission.
• **Delivered**: Confirmed delivery to contact's handset.
• **Seen**: Contact has opened the secure live-location tracking link.
• **Responding**: Contact has tapped "I'm on my way" or "Called Police 112".

Never wonder if help is coming. You see real-time updates directly on your distress dashboard.

---

### Core Safety Features:
🚨 **One-Touch & System-Wide SOS**:
Trigger instant distress from the main screen, lock screen notification, Home Screen Widget, or Quick Settings Tile without unlocking your phone.

⚡ **Shake & Impact Detection**:
Physics-based multi-directional accelerometer detection triggers if your phone is shaken violently or dropped during an assault. A 10-second cancelable countdown prevents false alarms.

🛡️ **Walk With Me Companion**:
Heading home alone or taking a cab late at night? Start a timed journey companion. If you miss a safety check-in, the app automatically alerts your circle with your live location.

📞 **Discreet Fake Call Generator**:
Quickly escape uncomfortable or threatening situations with a realistic simulated incoming phone call complete with ringtone and conversational audio.

🚨 **Direct 112 Emergency Integration**:
Instant connection to National Emergency Response Support System (ERSS 112) with automated Text-to-Speech location readout for dispatchers.

🔐 **Anti-Tamper & Secret Duress PIN**:
If an attacker forces you to cancel the alarm, entering your secret Duress PIN visually dismisses the screen while secretly streaming high-precision silent location updates to your family.

🔒 **Privacy by Design**:
Zero passive tracking. Your location is NEVER queried or shared when the app is inactive. Live tracking links automatically expire after your emergency is resolved.
