# Privacy Policy for I AM HERE

**Effective Date: September 2026**
**Version: 1.0.0**

**I AM HERE** ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal safety of women and vulnerable individuals. This Privacy Policy details the exact nature of data processed by the I AM HERE application and backend services.

---

### 1. Principle of Zero Passive Tracking
Your privacy is non-negotiable. **I AM HERE does not monitor, track, store, or transmit your location data when the application is idle.** 
Location services are engaged **ONLY** in two specific scenarios:
1. When you explicitly trigger an **Emergency SOS** (via tactile button, Quick Settings, lock screen, or verified violent shake/impact).
2. When you actively start a **Walk With Me** journey companion session.

As soon as an emergency or journey is concluded, the GPS receiver is powered down immediately.

---

### 2. Information We Process
During an active emergency distress signal, the app processes:
- **Precise Location Telemetry**: Latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, bearing, and accuracy radius to guide first responders and family members.
- **Battery & Telephony Diagnostics**: Battery level and cellular signal health to warn contacts if your phone is running out of power.
- **Trusted Circle Contacts**: Names, phone numbers, and relationships of people you explicitly designate to receive emergency alerts.
- **User Profile Data**: Your name, emergency phone number, blood group, and optional medical notes (e.g. allergies) displayed to emergency helpers.

---

### 3. Local Storage of Security PINs
Your **Master Cancellation PIN** and **Secret Duress PIN** are stored locally on your device in protected private storage. They are never sent in plaintext over the internet.

---

### 4. Public Live-Location Tracking Links & Automatic Expiration
- When an SOS is triggered, a secure, cryptographically random tracking token is generated.
- Only individuals who possess the unique link (your designated emergency contacts) can view your live coordinates.
- **Token Expiration**: Live tracking links automatically expire **2 hours** after an emergency session is marked resolved, or within **24 hours** maximum. Once expired, the link returns HTTP 404 and is permanently inaccessible.

---

### 5. Third-Party Sharing & Commercialization
- **We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, location history, or contacts.**
- Data is transmitted exclusively to:
  - Your explicitly designated emergency contacts via SMS and Web Push.
  - Emergency dispatchers (112 ERSS) when you dial emergency services.
  - Legally mandated law enforcement agencies strictly in active missing-persons investigations under applicable law.

---

### 6. User Rights & Data Erasure
You have the complete right to view, modify, or delete your profile, contacts, and historical sessions at any time directly within the application settings.
